## Direct answer

A blank display with a house breaker that appears on still needs a spa-side power check. Jacuzzi tells owners facing no power to confirm the main breaker and spa GFCI are on and to check that any exterior disconnect has not been turned off (Jacuzzi). Hot Spring also documents a black wireless touchscreen when spa power is off or the remote battery needs charging (Hot Spring).

## Safety note

Do not open the control box. Do not touch wiring. Do not probe fuses, transformers, or ribbon cables. Reset a GFCI or breaker once only if your manual allows it. If it will not reset, or trips again, stop. Jacuzzi treats an immediate re-trip as a persistent electrical fault that needs a licensed electrician (Jacuzzi).

## Why the house breaker can look fine

The breaker you flipped in the garage is not necessarily the last switch in the spa circuit. Jacuzzi's electrical guide notes that an exterior disconnect can be off for maintenance or by someone unfamiliar with the system, even when owners expect the spa to have power (Jacuzzi). Hot Spring Limelight service guidance likewise tells owners to check spa GFCI breakers in the subpanel and the house breaker that supplies the spa before assuming a deeper fault (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual).

A blank topside is therefore a location problem first: confirm power is actually reaching the spa equipment path your manual describes.

Use this article when the screen or LEDs stay dark.

Use different guides when:

- You need the full inoperative-spa triage: [Why Is My Hot Tub Control Panel Not Working?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-control-panel-not-working)
- The spa runs briefly, then dies: [Why Does My Hot Tub Keep Turning Off?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-keep-turning-off)

Jacuzzi's pump guide also separates "no power to the entire spa" from a spa where lights or the control panel still illuminate (Jacuzzi). That distinction matters here: if anything else on the spa still has life, you are not in a pure blank-display power-loss case.

## Checks that stay outside the control box

### Confirm spa GFCI and disconnect

1. Find the spa GFCI or subpanel your installer labeled for the hot tub.
2. Confirm it is fully on, following the reset method in your manual.
3. Check any exterior disconnect near the spa (Jacuzzi).
4. If a GFCI trips again immediately after one reset, leave it off and call qualified service (Jacuzzi; Hot Spring).

### Allow a documented high-limit reset path

If the spa failed after overheating or low flow, Hot Spring Limelight guidance says to turn power off for about 30 seconds to reset a tripped high-limit thermostat, then look for clogged filters and related flow problems if the spa restarts (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). That is not a license to open the pack and press internal buttons unless your own manual names that exact step.

### Wireless or docked panels

Hot Spring's chart for a control touchscreen that is off and black lists spa power off, a remote battery that needs recharging, Shut-Down mode, and a battery that is not recharging (Hot Spring). Documented homeowner steps include turning spa power on, placing the remote in the docking tray to recharge, and pressing the touchscreen then waiting about 40 seconds (Hot Spring). Call an electrician if spa power will not turn on, or spa service if the battery will not recharge (Hot Spring).

These wireless steps apply only to spa systems that use that remote design. Fixed topsides do not have a "dock to recharge" path.

## What a blank screen does not prove

A dark display does not identify a failed topside membrane, a bad control board, or a specific heater fault. Hot Spring Limelight documentation notes that an off Power indicator can point to a problem with the spa control box or the power supplied to the spa, and then refers owners to troubleshooting rather than homeowner board replacement (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual).

Do not diagnose internal parts from darkness alone.

## When to stop and call a technician

- Spa GFCI or disconnect checks do not restore the display
- The protective device trips again after one allowed reset
- The remote will not recharge or stay paired after the steps in your manual
- Water, burning smells, or damaged cords are present near the spa power path
- The next step would require opening the control box or testing conductors

If the panel returns but the water will not heat, switch to [Hot Tub Not Heating: What to Check First](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-not-heating).
