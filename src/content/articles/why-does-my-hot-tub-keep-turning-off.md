## Direct answer

If the spa starts, then dies again, treat it as a repeating protective trip, not as a permanently dead panel. Hot Spring documents a blinking Power indicator as a tripped heater high-limit thermostat and instructs owners to disconnect power for about 30 seconds, check clogged filters, and call for service if the thermostat keeps tripping (Hot Spring). Jacuzzi treats a GFCI that trips again immediately after reset as an electrical fault that needs a licensed electrician, not more reset attempts (Jacuzzi).

## Safety note

Do not open the control box. Do not bypass a high-limit thermostat, GFCI, pressure switch, or thermal cut-off. Do not keep resetting a protective device that will not hold. One reset only when your owner's manual directs that exact step. If the device trips again, leave power off and call qualified service.

## "Keeps turning off" is not "never turns on"

A spa that is dark from the first look belongs under [Why Is My Hot Tub Control Panel Not Working?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-control-panel-not-working) or [Hot Tub Display Is Blank but the Breaker Is On](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-display-is-blank-but-the-breaker-is-on).

This article is for intermittent or repeating shutdowns: the spa runs, then the panel goes dark, the GFCI trips, jets stop, or a Power indicator starts blinking after a period of operation.

## Manufacturer-documented reasons the spa can shut down again

### Heater high-limit thermostat trip

Hot Spring Limelight manuals explain that removing spa power for about 30 seconds can reset a tripped high-limit thermostat. If the spa then energizes, reduced water flow through the heating system is indicated. Documented contributors include clogged filter cartridges, plumbing blockage, a non-functioning heater circulation pump, draining the spa without disconnecting power first, and an air lock in the plumbing lines (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). The Power indicator on the control panel can blink when that high-limit circuit has tripped (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual; Hot Spring).

If high-limit trips keep returning after the filter and flow checks your manual allows, Hot Spring tells owners to call for service (Hot Spring).

For the filter-and-heat relationship in more detail, see [Can a Dirty Filter Stop a Hot Tub From Heating?](/troubleshooting/can-a-dirty-filter-stop-a-hot-tub-from-heating).

### GFCI re-trip

Hot Spring lists a tripped GFCI among causes when the spa is inoperative and says to call for service if the GFCI will not reset (Hot Spring). Jacuzzi separates occasional trips during use, which may need the spa shut down and scheduled service, from an immediate re-trip after reset, which should go to a licensed electrician (Jacuzzi).

Repeated GFCI trips are not a homeowner board-swap project.

### Pump thermal protection

Hot Spring Limelight service information notes that a jet pump can shut itself off on thermal overload. On a newer spa, documented contributors include a sensitive thermal cutoff, high temperature from a blocked pump shroud vent, break-in friction, or improper wiring such as an extension cord or undersized house wiring (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). Owners are told to clear vent blockage and ensure equipment-compartment ventilation, then contact a qualified technician if the pump continues shutting off after short use (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual).

That guidance is model-family specific. Follow your own manual's pump-protection section rather than assuming every brand uses the same vent layout.

## Safe checks before another reset

1. **Write down what turns off:** whole spa, jets only, heat only, or GFCI alone.
2. **Note any blinking Power or Ready indicator** before you cut power (Hot Spring).
3. **Inspect or clean filters** only by your manual's method if high-limit or flow trips are documented for your spa (Hot Spring).
4. **Confirm water level** against the fill mark in your manual.
5. **Apply one power-off interval** only if your manual uses that step to reset high-limit, then restore power once.
6. **Stop** if the same trip returns.

If heat never returns while the panel stays alive, use [Hot Tub Not Heating: What to Check First](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-not-heating) instead of treating every heat loss as a full spa shutdown.

## Panel messages during shutdowns

Some spas show a temperature code, flashing icons, or dashes when a safety path engages. Those symbols are brand- and manual-specific. Do not invent a universal meaning. Use [Hot Tub Control Panel Is Flashing or Showing Dashes](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-control-panel-is-flashing-or-showing-dashes) and your printed error section.

## When to stop and call a technician

- High-limit or similar trips return after filter cleaning and the power-cycle your manual allows
- The GFCI or breaker trips again after one reset
- The pump keeps shutting off after short operation once vents are clear
- Burning smells, scorched cords, or water in the equipment area appear
- The next step would require opening the control box or testing electrical parts

Repeated shutdowns after the documented homeowner checks are a service call, not a longer DIY loop.
