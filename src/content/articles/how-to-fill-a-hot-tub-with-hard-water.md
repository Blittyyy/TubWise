Filling a hot tub with hard water is about preparation before chemistry catch-up. Hot Spring states that metals and calcium need to be managed as you fill the spa to help prevent residue on surfaces and unwanted chemical interactions ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-choose-the-right-spa-chemicals-for-my-hot-tub)). Hot Spring's calcium FAQ also recommends an On the Go portable water softener for filling and topping off when water has heavy minerals and calcium hardness above 150 ppm, and a water softener when calcium hardness is above 300 ppm ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-reduce-calcium-hardness-my-spa-water)).

Those thresholds and product names are Hot Spring guidance. Confirm your owner's manual, your sanitizer or salt system, and a source-water test before choosing any treatment.

If scale is already on the shell, see [Why Is There Scale on My Hot Tub Shell?](/water-care/why-is-there-scale-on-my-hot-tub-shell). If seats feel sandpaper-rough, see [Why Does My Hot Tub Surface Feel Rough or Sandy?](/water-care/why-does-my-hot-tub-surface-feel-rough-or-sandy).

## Safety note

Do not power heating equipment dry. Follow your spa's drain and fill procedure for power-off, fill path, and start-up order. Do not mix different pool or spa chemicals. Do not invent doses. Use product labels, known spa volume, current test results, and your manual together. If heat fails after a refill, see [Hot Tub Not Heating After a Refill](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-not-heating-after-a-refill).

## Before you open the hose

1. **Test the source water or take a sample to your dealer.** A fill plan without a reading is guesswork. FreshWater Salt System start-up materials tell owners to have fill water checked for metals, phosphates, and calcium when that system applies ([Hot Spring FreshWater Salt Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/freshwater-salt-system-owners-manual.pdf)).
2. **Read your spa manual's fill section.** Hot Spring Highlife guidance allows filling with softened water only if calcium hardness parameters in that manual are maintained, and it warns that care is needed when filling from a water softener so calcium stays in balance ([Hot Spring Highlife Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/highlife-owners-manual-en_us.pdf)).
3. **Identify your water-care system.** FreshWater Salt System calcium bands and metal start-up steps differ from standard Hot Spring care ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-choose-the-right-spa-chemicals-for-my-hot-tub); [Hot Spring FreshWater Salt Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/freshwater-salt-system-owners-manual.pdf)). Do not apply salt-system steps to a spa that does not use that system.

## Fill precautions Hot Spring documents

### Pre-filter for metals and organics

Hot Spring describes Clean Screen as a hose-end pre-filter that removes metals such as copper and iron, plus organic contaminants and tannins that can discolor water or contribute to foam ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-choose-the-right-spa-chemicals-for-my-hot-tub)). The Highlife manual recommends Clean Screen at every fill and top-off for iron, copper, and manganese ([Hot Spring Highlife Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/highlife-owners-manual-en_us.pdf)).

A pre-filter is not a claim that every mineral or metal is removed. Hot Spring still treats calcium reduction as a separate step with softener or Vanishing Act tools in its calcium FAQ ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-reduce-calcium-hardness-my-spa-water)).

### Softener on the hose for high calcium

For heavy minerals and calcium hardness above 150 ppm, Hot Spring recommends On the Go for fill and top-off ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-reduce-calcium-hardness-my-spa-water)). The spa-chemicals FAQ states that On the Go removes calcium as water flows through, and that softener output can drop calcium below the recommended level, so owners should test the water coming out and, if needed, finish filling with water straight from the hose ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-choose-the-right-spa-chemicals-for-my-hot-tub)). FreshWater Salt System manuals likewise call for Clean Screen and/or On The Go when fill water has high metal or calcium levels stated in that manual ([Hot Spring FreshWater Salt Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/freshwater-salt-system-owners-manual.pdf)).

### Fill path and water source

Hot Spring Highlife fill instructions say to fill through the filter compartment and not to fill with swimming-pool water, because pool chemicals differ from spa chemicals and can damage the spa ([Hot Spring Highlife Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/highlife-owners-manual-en_us.pdf)).

If you need a planning estimate for how long the remaining fill may take once you know capacity and hose flow, use the [Hot Tub Fill Time Calculator](/calculators/hot-tub-fill-time-calculator). Treat the result as an estimate only.

## After the spa is full

1. **Test calcium hardness, pH, alkalinity, and sanitizer** with a kit that matches your system.
2. **Compare results to your manual, not to a universal TubWise table.** Hot Spring publishes its own standard and salt-system hardness ranges in brand materials ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-choose-the-right-spa-chemicals-for-my-hot-tub)). Other brands may differ.
3. **If calcium is still high after fill, use only the calcium-reduction method your brand documents.** Hot Spring's calcium FAQ describes Vanishing Act placement after testing and points to the owner's manual for exact placement ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-reduce-calcium-hardness-my-spa-water)). Follow that product's instructions. Do not invent a dose for a different brand.
4. **If metals are confirmed or strongly suspected, follow metal-treatment sequencing for your system.** Hot Spring's metals FAQ describes Clean Screen fill, then FreshWater Stain & Scale, then a 24-hour wait before chlorine or other oxidizers ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/faq/how-do-i-reduce-metals-my-water)). FreshWater Salt System manuals add system-off and filter-cleaning steps during metal treatment; follow that manual if it applies.
5. **Balance and sanitize only after any required waits**, using labeled products one at a time as your start-up guide directs.

## When to stop and get help

Stop filling or stop adding start-up chemicals if you cannot confirm manual steps, if softener output drives calcium below your manual's minimum, if metal or calcium readings remain outside the ranges your brand requires after labeled tools, or if discoloration or heat problems appear after refill. Contact your dealer or a qualified spa technician with your source-water test results and spa model information.
