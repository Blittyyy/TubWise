## Direct answer

If the panel still shows power or a temperature, but presses do nothing, start with spa lock. Hot Spring lists an activated spa lock as a cause of an inoperative spa and states that spa lock disables the controls on the control panel (Hot Spring; Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). On certain wireless Hot Spring systems, a lit but inoperable touchscreen can also mean the remote is too far away or communication with the spa was interrupted (Hot Spring).

## Safety note

Do not open the control box. Do not peel the keypad, spray cleaners into the panel, or probe ribbon cables. Do not bypass locks, sensors, or safety devices. If an error code or dashes appear instead of a normal display, stop button troubleshooting and match the exact message in your own manual. Jacuzzi advises looking up panel error codes in the owner's manual and calling professional repair when that path does not resolve the issue (Jacuzzi).

## Lit panel that ignores presses is not a blank panel

This article assumes the topside still shows something: digits, icons, a lock symbol, or a lit wireless screen.

If the display is fully dark while the house breaker looks on, use [Hot Tub Display Is Blank but the Breaker Is On](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-display-is-blank-but-the-breaker-is-on) first. For the broader inoperative-spa list, see [Why Is My Hot Tub Control Panel Not Working?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-control-panel-not-working).

## Spa lock and temperature lock

Hot Spring Limelight manuals document Spa Lock and Temperature Lock as separate features. Spa Lock disables the controls on the control panel, and the jet pumps will not function while Spa Lock is active (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). A lock indicator icon can show when Spa Lock, Temperature Lock, or both are on (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual).

Hot Spring's consumer chart also lists spa lock among causes when the spa is inoperative, with the solution stated as deactivating spa lock (Hot Spring).

Follow only the unlock sequence printed for your model. On Limelight examples, Spa Lock is deactivated by a documented soft-button and hold sequence that turns the spa lock icon off (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). Other brands use different button holds. Do not invent a universal unlock shortcut.

Temperature Lock alone may still allow some functions while blocking temperature changes. Your manual draws that line; this article does not replace it.

## Wireless touchscreen still on but inoperable

Hot Spring's chart for a control touchscreen that is on but inoperable lists two causes: the remote is too far from the spa, or communication between the control system and the remote was interrupted (Hot Spring). Documented steps include moving the remote closer, pressing the Jets button on the dock unit, and, if a panel reset is needed, pressing and holding the touchscreen in a blank area until it turns black (Hot Spring).

Those steps apply to Hot Spring wireless remote systems described in that chart. Fixed membrane panels do not use dock-range troubleshooting.

## What not to assume

Unresponsive buttons do not prove the membrane failed, the main board failed, or the heater failed. They also do not prove a specific error code. If the display is flashing, showing dashes, or naming a code, leave this path and use [Hot Tub Control Panel Is Flashing or Showing Dashes](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-control-panel-is-flashing-or-showing-dashes) plus your brand manual.

If jets still fail after spa lock is clearly off, return to flow and heating guides such as [Hot Tub Not Heating: What to Check First](/troubleshooting/hot-tub-not-heating) rather than forcing more panel resets.

## Safe checks the homeowner can do

1. **Confirm the display is actually lit** and note any lock icon, code, or dashes.
2. **Deactivate spa lock** only with the method in your owner's manual (Hot Spring).
3. **If you have a wireless remote,** move it closer to the spa and follow your manual's dock or reset steps (Hot Spring).
4. **Try one power cycle at the spa disconnect** only if your manual uses that step to clear a stuck panel state.
5. **Stop** if the next idea is opening the equipment pack or replacing the topside without a technician.

## When to stop and call a technician

- Spa lock is off according to your manual, and presses still do nothing
- Wireless pairing or dock steps in your manual fail
- Moisture, cracked overlays, or burnt smells appear at the topside
- An error code, Watchdog-style dashes, or repeating protective trip remains after the manual's allowed reset
- You would need electrical testing or control-box access to continue
