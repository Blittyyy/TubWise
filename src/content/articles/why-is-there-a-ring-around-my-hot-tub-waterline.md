A ring around the hot tub waterline is often surface film from oils and dirt collecting where air meets water. Hot Spring's troubleshooting chart lists **organic buildup / scum ring around spa** as a build-up of oils and dirt, and directs owners to wipe the scum with a clean rag; if severe, drain the spa, use a spa surface and tile cleaner, and refill ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/owners/water-care-and-troubleshooting)).

That is different from green or brown metal staining on the shell, which Hot Spring links to high iron or copper in source water ([Hot Spring Highlife Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/highlife-owners-manual-en_us.pdf)). It is also different from cloudy water throughout the spa; see [Why Is My Hot Tub Water Cloudy?](/water-care/why-is-my-hot-tub-water-cloudy).

## Safety note

Use only cleaners approved for your spa shell. Harsh household cleaners, abrasive pads, and solvents can damage acrylic and void warranties. For shell-safe methods, see [How to Clean a Hot Tub Shell Without Damaging It](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-shell-without-damaging-it). Follow chemical product labels. Do not mix cleaners with spa sanitizers in the water. If you drain for a deep clean, follow your spa's drain and refill procedure before adding chemicals again.

## Causes to check

### Oils and dirt from bathers

Hot Spring attributes the scum ring to oils and dirt ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/owners/water-care-and-troubleshooting)). Lotions, body oils, and similar residues float and collect at the waterline. Soap and detergent residue can also leave film and foam; see [Can Soap, Lotion, or Detergent Cause Hot Tub Foam?](/water-care/can-soap-lotion-or-detergent-cause-hot-tub-foam).

### Oils and minerals concentrating at the waterline

Jacuzzi's drain and clean guidance calls out the waterline as the place where oils and minerals concentrate, and its cleaning guidance recommends wiping the waterline with a soft cloth during regular maintenance to help prevent buildup ([Jacuzzi](https://www.jacuzzi.com/en-us/How-to-Drain-a-Spa.html); [Jacuzzi](https://www.jacuzzi.com/en-us/How-to-Clean-a-Hot-Tub.html)). A hard, crusty band can involve mineral scale as well as soft organic film. Do not assume every ring is only "scum." If the deposit feels hard and chalky, check calcium hardness and pH per your manual before aggressive scrubbing, and see [Why Is There Scale on My Hot Tub Shell?](/water-care/why-is-there-scale-on-my-hot-tub-shell). If the surface feels sandpaper-rough more than ring-like, see [Why Does My Hot Tub Surface Feel Rough or Sandy?](/water-care/why-does-my-hot-tub-surface-feel-rough-or-sandy).

### Stains that are not a scum ring

Hot Spring notes that high iron or copper in the water may produce green or brown stains on the spa, which is a different problem from an oil-and-dirt scum ring ([Hot Spring Highlife Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/highlife-owners-manual-en_us.pdf)). Colored staining that returns after wiping may need metal treatment guidance from your dealer and manual, not only waterline wiping.

### Filtration and water age

Organics that the filter does not remove can keep feeding a waterline film. Keep the filter cleaned on schedule; see [How to Clean a Hot Tub Filter](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-filter). Water that has been in the spa a long time can also make film harder to control. See [How Often Should You Drain and Refill a Hot Tub?](/maintenance/how-often-should-you-drain-and-refill-a-hot-tub).

## Safe checks you can do

1. **Wipe a small section with a clean soft rag.** Hot Spring's first step for a scum ring is wiping with a clean rag ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/owners/water-care-and-troubleshooting)). Soft cloth only.
2. **Note texture and color.** Soft brownish film points toward oils and dirt. Hard white crust points toward mineral scale. Green or brown staining on the shell may involve metals ([Hot Spring Highlife Manual](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/highlife-owners-manual-en_us.pdf)).
3. **Test the water.** Check sanitizer, pH, alkalinity, and hardness. Unbalanced water can make deposits worse even when wiping helps short-term.
4. **Use a spa-approved surface cleaner only if wiping is not enough.** Hot Spring's severe-case path is drain, spa surface and tile cleaner, then refill ([Hot Spring](https://www.hotspring.com/owners/water-care-and-troubleshooting)). Match the cleaner to your shell material and brand guidance.
5. **Clean during a planned drain if the ring is severe.** Jacuzzi treats waterline cleaning as part of a full drain-and-clean routine ([Jacuzzi](https://www.jacuzzi.com/en-us/How-to-Drain-a-Spa.html)).
6. **Reduce what enters the water.** A rinse-off shower before soaking reduces lotions and oils. Keep the cover clean so dirt does not wash into the waterline when you open it.

## When to stop and get help

Stop scrubbing and stop guessing cleaners if:

- The ring will not come off with a soft rag and a spa-approved cleaner used as labeled
- You see green or brown staining that looks like metal deposits rather than soft film
- The shell shows dulling, scratches, or soft spots after cleaning attempts
- Odor, slime on surfaces, or cloudy water accompanies the ring and chemistry will not stabilize ([Why Does My Hot Tub Water Smell Bad?](/water-care/why-does-my-hot-tub-water-smell-bad))

Contact your dealer or a qualified spa technician for shell damage, persistent metal staining, or water that will not stay balanced after a drain and refill.
