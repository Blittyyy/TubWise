## Direct answer

A workable hot tub maintenance checklist mixes short daily checks with weekly water care, monthly filter attention, and seasonal drain-and-clean work. Hot Spring's maintenance schedule lists daily power and cover checks, weekly water testing and skimming, monthly filter rinsing, and a seasonal drain about every three to four months for traditional water care (Hot Spring). Jacuzzi's beginner guide likewise groups weekly chemistry checks and a full drain every three to four months, or sooner when total dissolved solids exceed 2,000 ppm in that guide (Jacuzzi).

## Safety note

Follow chemical product labels and your spa manual for dosing. Do not mix sanitizer systems. Do not open the control box or work on wiring during routine care. Turn power off before filter removal if your manual requires it. Stop and call qualified service for electrical faults, persistent leaks, or tasks your manual marks as dealer work.

## How to use this checklist

Treat the intervals below as a planning frame from published manufacturer schedules. Exact filter methods, cover cleaners, and drain procedures vary by brand and model. When Hot Spring and Jacuzzi differ, use your own owner's manual as the final rule for your spa.

## Daily checks

Hot Spring lists daily tasks that take little time (Hot Spring):

- Confirm the spa stays powered so water can circulate and temperature can hold
- Check that the cover is firmly latched and locked when the spa is not in use

Daily circulation matters because Hot Spring describes filtration that depends on water moving through the filters even when nobody is soaking (Hot Spring). Programming details belong in your manual.

## Weekly tasks

Hot Spring's weekly list includes testing pH, alkalinity, and sanitizer, then adjusting chemicals to the ranges your manufacturer recommends, plus skimming the surface for dust and oil (Hot Spring). Jacuzzi's ongoing-maintenance section tells owners to test chemical levels twice weekly and adjust as needed (Jacuzzi).

Public-health testing guidance for chlorine and pH can be stricter than a weekly brand checklist. See [How Often Should You Test Hot Tub Water?](/maintenance/how-often-should-you-test-hot-tub-water) before treating any single cadence as universal.

Weekly checklist:

1. Test sanitizer and balance parameters your manual tracks
2. Adjust only with products and amounts your label and manual allow
3. Skim floating debris if present
4. Confirm water level against your fill mark

## Monthly tasks

Hot Spring lists a monthly filter rinse with a hose so cartridges stay unclogged, and notes shocking when the spa sees regular use, with product choice referred to the dealer (Hot Spring). Hot Spring Highlife manuals also tell owners to clean filter cartridges monthly (Hot Spring Highlife owner's manual). Jacuzzi describes monthly deep cleaning of dirty filters in filter-cleaner solution as part of setup and care guidance (Jacuzzi).

For brand-by-brand rinse and soak intervals, use [How Often Should You Clean a Hot Tub Filter?](/maintenance/how-often-should-you-clean-a-hot-tub-filter) and [How to Clean a Hot Tub Filter](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-filter) rather than repeating those methods here.

Hot Spring and Jacuzzi also point owners to monthly vinyl cover cleaning in their cover-care materials. See [How to Clean a Hot Tub Cover](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-cover).

## Seasonal or quarterly tasks

Hot Spring groups these under seasonal or quarterly care (Hot Spring):

- Deep-clean filters with a filter cleaner product your brand accepts
- Drain and change spa water about every three to four months on traditional water care; Hot Spring notes salt-system spas may go about a year when properly maintained
- Wipe the empty shell with a mild, non-abrasive approach after draining, and clean pillows with mild soap and water

Jacuzzi's beginner guide also schedules a complete drain and refill every three to four months, or when TDS exceeds 2,000 ppm (Jacuzzi). Details and caveats live in [How Often Should You Drain and Refill a Hot Tub?](/maintenance/how-often-should-you-drain-and-refill-a-hot-tub). Shell wipe-down rules differ by brand; see [How to Clean a Hot Tub Shell Without Damaging It](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-shell-without-damaging-it).

## Annual tasks Hot Spring lists

Hot Spring's annual list includes flushing plumbing lines (also allowed each drain-and-refill), a dealer or professional check of hardware and controls, a cover inspection for mildew, moisture, and damage, and winterizing only when your climate and absence plan require it (Hot Spring). Do not invent winterizing steps from this checklist alone. If the cover fails that inspection, see [When Should You Replace a Hot Tub Cover?](/maintenance/when-should-you-replace-a-hot-tub-cover).

## What this checklist does not replace

- Your model-specific owner's manual
- Chemical label directions
- Dealer service for electrical, structural, or persistent water-quality failures

Use the list to remember the cadence. Use the linked guides and your manual for the exact how-to.
