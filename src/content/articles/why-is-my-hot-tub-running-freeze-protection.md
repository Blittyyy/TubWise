## Direct answer

If the spa starts pumps on its own in cold weather, or the panel shows freeze/ICE language, that can be normal protection rather than a failed control. Jacuzzi J-400 manuals describe ICE as a freeze condition that needs no corrective action while pumps operate until the spa is out of danger (Jacuzzi). Leave power on so the spa can keep water moving. Shutting the spa down for long periods in a hard freeze removes that protection.

## Safety note

Do not cut power for long stretches during freezing weather unless your manufacturer or dealer is winterizing the spa. Do not open the control box or test temperature sensors with a meter. Do not assume every automatic pump run is a stuck relay. If freeze protection is accompanied by a GFCI trip, burning smell, or no water movement while ICE is displayed, leave the spa safe and call qualified service.

## Normal freeze protection versus a real fault

| What you see | What it usually means | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold weather, pumps start alone, water is moving | Freeze protection working | Leave power on; let the cycle finish (Jacuzzi) |
| Panel shows ICE or similar freeze wording | Documented freeze condition on supported systems | No action required while protection runs (Jacuzzi) |
| Quiet low-speed circulation while heating or filtering | Normal automatic operation | See [Why Won't My Hot Tub Jets Turn Off?](/troubleshooting/why-wont-my-hot-tub-jets-turn-off) |
| High-speed jets stuck on and Off does nothing in mild weather | Abnormal stuck-on path | Use the jets-won't-turn-off guide; cut power if needed |
| Spa keeps shutting down rather than protecting itself | Shutdown path | [Why Does My Hot Tub Keep Turning Off?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-keep-turning-off) |
| GFCI trips during cold-weather run | Electrical fault path | [Why Does My Hot Tub GFCI Keep Tripping?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-gfci-keep-tripping) |

### Why freeze protection exists

Moving water is harder to freeze than standing water in pipes and the heater. Jacuzzi J-200 manuals describe a freeze-protection feature that helps maintain a minimum water temperature when the spa is kept in operation, and they warn owners to confirm the electrical supply has not been interrupted in severe cold (Jacuzzi). Jacuzzi J-400 manuals separately describe ICE as a potential freeze condition with pumps running until the spa is out of danger (Jacuzzi).

Balboa M7 support documentation describes ICE when the water temperature inside the heater drops below the system's freeze threshold, with equipment activating automatically until sensors recover (SpaGuts / Balboa M7). Thresholds and restart rules are brand-specific. Confirm the exact message in your own manual.

### Why you generally should not shut power off

Freeze protection cannot run without power. Jacuzzi winter guidance emphasizes checking that electrical supply remains available during severe freezing temperatures when the spa is left in service (Jacuzzi). If you need a long absence in hard freeze conditions, follow your manufacturer's winterizing path or dealer service rather than simply flipping the breaker and leaving water in the plumbing.

### Manufacturer wording is scoped

ICE language in this article is taken from Jacuzzi J-400 and Balboa M7 documentation. Other brands may use different words or no code at all while still circulating for freeze protection. Do not invent a universal code meaning.

## Safe checks the homeowner can do

1. **Note outdoor temperature and whether the spa is actually cold.**
2. **Read the panel.** Write down ICE, freeze, filter, or heat indicators exactly.
3. **Confirm water is moving** while protection is active. Displayed protection with no pump sound and no flow is a service call.
4. **Leave power on** during genuine cold-weather protection (Jacuzzi).
5. **Do not treat temporary manual pump-off as a permanent cancel.** Jacuzzi notes that pumps can return if freeze is still detected after a manual off attempt (Jacuzzi).
6. **If the spa is losing temperature for other reasons,** use [Why Does My Hot Tub Temperature Keep Dropping?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-temperature-keep-dropping). Overheat or high-limit behavior belongs on [Why Is My Hot Tub Getting Too Hot?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-getting-too-hot).

For seasonal planning around covers, filters, and drain timing, see [Hot Tub Maintenance Checklist: Weekly, Monthly, and Seasonal Tasks](/maintenance/hot-tub-maintenance-checklist-weekly-monthly-and-seasonal-tasks).

## When freeze behavior looks abnormal

Call service, or follow the linked electrical guides, if:

- Automatic high-intensity pump run continues in warm weather with no freeze indication
- ICE or freeze wording never clears after temperatures recover
- The panel claims protection but no water moves
- A GFCI trip, burning smell, or scorched equipment appears during the cycle
- Water temperature cannot be maintained and winter cover/filtration checks your manual allows are already done

Do not winterize from this page alone. Drain-down antifreeze steps, if needed, belong in your owner's manual or with a dealer.
