## Direct answer

Jets that keep running are not automatically a failed relay. Many spas run a low-speed pump for filtration or heating without anyone pressing Jets, and freeze protection can start pumps on its own in cold conditions (Canadian Spa Company; Jacuzzi). A true stuck-on problem is different: high-speed therapy jets keep running and the topside cannot stop them. In that case, shut the spa off at the breaker and call a qualified spa technician.

## Safety note

Do not open the control box. Do not tap, jump, or replace relays. Do not bypass safety devices. If high-speed jets will not stop from the panel, leave the breaker off until a technician can inspect the spa. Keep people out of the water while the spa behaves unpredictably.

## Normal automatic operation versus genuinely stuck on

Use this fork before you assume the control pack failed:

| What you see | What it often is | Next path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet low-speed circulation with no one pressing Jets | Filtration cycle or heating circulation | Check filter/heat indicators in your manual (Canadian Spa Company) |
| Pumps start on their own in cold weather, or the panel shows freeze/ICE language | Freeze protection | See [Why Is My Hot Tub Running Freeze Protection?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-running-freeze-protection) |
| Jets button cycles low → high → off on a normal schedule | Timed jet operation | Wait for the documented timeout, or press Jets again per your manual (Canadian Spa Company) |
| Display lit, but Off / Jets presses do nothing | Control lock or unresponsive panel | See [Why Are My Hot Tub Buttons Not Responding?](/troubleshooting/why-are-my-hot-tub-buttons-not-responding) |
| High-speed jets keep running and the panel cannot stop them | Abnormal stuck-on condition | Cut power at the breaker; call service |

### Circulation or filtration is not the same as stuck therapy jets

Canadian Spa Company explains that the low-speed pump can run for filtration cycles and for heating even when nobody presses Jets, and that Ready mode can start low-speed circulation periodically to check temperature (Canadian Spa Company). That quiet automatic run is different from high-speed massage jets that refuse to shut off.

If the main complaint is sound while jets are intentionally on, use [Why Does My Hot Tub Make Noise When the Jets Are On?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-make-noise-when-the-jets-are-on) instead of this stuck-on path.

### Freeze protection can look like “it won’t turn off”

Jacuzzi J-400 manuals describe an ICE freeze condition in which pumps operate until the spa is out of danger, and note that manually turning pumps off may not permanently cancel freeze protection if the condition remains (Jacuzzi). Cold-weather auto-run belongs on the freeze-protection guide, not on a stuck-relay assumption.

### Unresponsive Off commands

If the panel is lit but ignores Off or Jets presses, confirm spa lock and topside response first. Hot Spring Limelight manuals document Spa Lock disabling control-panel functions and jet-pump operation while the lock is active (Hot Spring Limelight owner's manual). Follow only your model's unlock sequence.

## Safe checks the homeowner can do

1. **Look at the panel indicators.** Note filter, heat, freeze/ICE, or lock icons before you cut power.
2. **Press Jets through the full cycle** your manual describes (often low, high, then off) and wait for any documented timeout (Canadian Spa Company).
3. **Decide whether the run is quiet low-speed circulation or loud high-speed therapy jets.**
4. **If it is cold outside and pumps started on their own,** use the freeze-protection path before treating it as a fault.
5. **If buttons are ignored,** leave this page and use the buttons-not-responding guide.
6. **If high-speed jets stay on and the panel cannot stop them,** turn the spa off at the breaker or disconnect and leave it off.

A short power cycle can clear temporary control glitches on some systems, but only after you have ruled out freeze protection and only if your manual allows restoring power afterward. If high-speed jets restart immediately and still ignore Off, leave power off.

Long automatic run also raises operating cost. For energy context after the spa is behaving normally again, see the [Hot Tub Electricity Cost Calculator](/calculators/hot-tub-electricity-cost-calculator).

## When to stop and call a technician

- High-speed jets restart or stay on after the panel Off commands fail
- You smell burning plastic, hear repeated clicking from the equipment bay, or see smoke
- Freeze protection is not active, filtration is not indicated, and the pump still will not stop
- The next step would require opening the spa pack or testing relays

A spa that keeps shutting down rather than staying on belongs on [Why Does My Hot Tub Keep Turning Off?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-keep-turning-off). Harsh pump noise after a stuck-on event belongs on [Why Is My Hot Tub Pump Making Noise?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-pump-making-noise) only once power is safely controlled.
