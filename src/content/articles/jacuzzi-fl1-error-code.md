## Exact scoped meaning

In the cited **Jacuzzi Premium ProTech LED** owner's manual for the **J-310, J-320, J-330, and J-340** models, a flashing **FL1** display means the flow switch is malfunctioning open, the circulation pump’s filter cartridge is excessively dirty, or an **air lock** has occurred at the circulation pump intake. That manual states the heater deactivates and that jets pump #1 may also deactivate.

**Jacuzzi UK** consumer support describes a related display on spas that show it: the panel may alternate between **FL–1**, a scrolling **FLOW ERROR OPEN** message, and the water temperature, and it describes FL1 as something affecting water flow. Treat FL1, FL–1, and FLOW ERROR OPEN as **manual-specific display variants**, not as one label that automatically applies to every Jacuzzi spa.

This page is **not** about Sundance FL1, Gecko FLO, or any other brand’s flow code. Similar letters on another spa can mean something different.

## Scope warning

Use this article only if:

- your spa is covered by a Jacuzzi or Jacuzzi Premium manual that lists **FL1**, or by Jacuzzi UK support text that lists **FL–1** / **FLOW ERROR OPEN**, and
- the wording on your panel matches that document.

The ProTech LED FL1 steps below apply only to the **J-310–J-340** line in that 2002 manual. They do **not** apply to current Jacuzzi collections that use different diagnostics. For example, the official **2025 J-300** owner's manual documents other codes rather than FL1. If your panel shows FL2, FL3, FLO, a numeric code, or another flow message, follow that exact entry in **your** manual instead.

Confirm the exact display text and correction steps in your Jacuzzi owner's manual before you change anything.

## What the code can indicate

In the cited ProTech LED and service documents, FL1 is a **reported flow condition**, not proof that one part failed. Documented possibilities include:

1. An excessively dirty circulation-pump filter cartridge
2. An air lock at the circulation pump intake
3. A flow switch reported open while the circulation pump should be moving water (a sensing or switch issue, not automatically a failed heater)

The Jacuzzi service document also lists an obstructed, misaligned, or defective flow switch among possible causes. That does not mean the switch is the diagnosis. Owner checks stop at water level, filter, and documented air-lock steps. Switch testing is technician work.

## Safe owner-level checks

Do not combine steps from different manuals into one sequence unless **your** manual lists them together. These checks are **not** guaranteed fixes.

### If your spa matches the ProTech LED J-310–J-340 FL1 entry

That owner's manual’s FL1 correction steps are:

1. Check for proper water level (about **1 inch below the lowest pillow** in that manual).
2. Check for a clogged or excessively dirty circulation-pump filter cartridge. See [How to Clean a Hot Tub Filter](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-filter), then follow that Jacuzzi filter section.
3. Purge air lock as that manual describes: remove the circulation filter cartridge, place a garden hose into the circulation pump filter wall fitting, run water for about **30 seconds**, turn the water off, reinstall the filter, and check the spa.
4. If the problem persists, contact your authorized dealer.

That ProTech LED FL1 entry does **not** instruct a breaker reset as the correction. Do not add one. The same manual’s fill instructions warn that incorrect refill can trap air; follow its filter-fitting fill path when you drain later.

### If you are following Jacuzzi UK support for FL–1 / FLOW ERROR OPEN

That support page’s listed steps are:

1. Turn the power off.
2. Check that the water level is below the bottom of the pillows; add water if necessary.
3. Check filters and wash them if clogged.
4. If that does not solve the problem, contact Jacuzzi support or your dealer as that page directs.

Restore power only after those checks, as ordinary operation requires. Do **not** repeatedly cycle power if the message returns or a breaker/GFCI trips.

## What not to do

Do not open the control box. Do not inspect or touch wiring. Do not measure voltage or resistance. Do not jumper or bypass a flow switch. Do not adjust or remove a flow switch based only on the code. Do not treat FL1 as proof that one part failed. Do not use Sundance FL1 or Gecko FLO procedures on a Jacuzzi spa. Do not apply these FL1 steps to a Jacuzzi collection that uses different diagnostic codes.

## When to stop and call a technician

Stop and contact an authorized Jacuzzi dealer or qualified spa technician if:

- FL1 or FL–1 returns after the safe owner checks in the matching document
- a GFCI or breaker trips, will not reset, or trips again after one reset
- you smell burning, see scorching, damaged wiring, or leaks near electrical equipment
- the next step would require opening an electrical enclosure, testing a switch or sensor, or accessing wiring
- you need equipment access beyond ordinary filter access or the hose-purge step your owner manual documents
- you are unsure whether your model and manual match this FL1 / FL–1 scope

## How to confirm the code in the owner's manual

1. Find your model and serial information as Jacuzzi describes for your spa.
2. Download or open the matching Jacuzzi owner's manual.
3. Locate the error-message or troubleshooting section for **FL1**, **FL–1**, or **FLOW ERROR OPEN**.
4. Match the exact wording on your panel to that entry.
5. Follow only the owner steps listed there. Ignore third-party “universal FL1” guides that mix Jacuzzi and Sundance wording.
