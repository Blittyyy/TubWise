## Direct answer

Treat chlorine and bromine as separate sanitizer systems. Jacuzzi UK states that you must choose one product path or the other and must never combine them in a hot tub (Jacuzzi UK). Confirm what your spa owner's manual allows, then buy products and a test kit that match that system.

## Chemical safety

Follow the sanitizer product label, your spa volume, and the owner's manual. Do not invent dosing amounts from this page. Do not mix chlorine and bromine products. Keep chemicals in a cool, dry, ventilated area away from children and pets as product labels and manufacturer guidance describe. Jacuzzi's swim-spa guidance warns that household chemicals such as bleach or non-spa detergents can damage components and may void warranty coverage (Jacuzzi).

## How TubWise researched this guide

This guide is desk research based on manufacturer water-care documentation and CDC Healthy Swimming guidance. TubWise did not independently lab-test sanitizer products. The focus is category-level selection criteria, not naming a universal winner. Formulations, labels, and spa restrictions can change, so confirm current product and manual details before purchasing or switching systems.

## Why spas use a sanitizer

Hot tub water is reused, so it needs ongoing disinfection. Jacuzzi UK explains that chlorine or bromine is used to address bacteria and pathogens that can cause illness or irritation, and that additional systems such as UV do not remove the need for chemical sanitizer (Jacuzzi UK). CDC frames free chlorine concentration and pH together as the first defense against germs in home pools and hot tubs (CDC).

## Category differences that matter when choosing

### Form and use pattern

Jacuzzi UK describes chlorine as available in granular form or tablets, and describes bromine as another sanitizer option used for spa water (Jacuzzi UK). Use only the form your spa manual and product label allow. If your spa includes a brominator or feeder, use it only as that spa manufacturer documents.

Jacuzzi UK also describes chlorine as comparatively faster-reacting and bromine as more stable and slower in that comparison article (Jacuzzi UK). Those are Jacuzzi UK category contrasts, not proof that one brand product is universally stronger or better for every spa.

### Testing

You need a test method that matches the sanitizer in the water. See [What to Look for in a Hot Tub Water Test Kit](/buying-guides/what-to-look-for-in-a-hot-tub-water-test-kit). Jacuzzi's beginner guide tells owners to test before soaking and to follow the sanitizer system they chose (Jacuzzi). Use the target ranges in your own manual and product labels. Do not treat odor alone as proof of correct sanitizer balance.

### Shock and oxidizer choices

Jacuzzi UK recommends non-chlorine shock in a bromine tub because chlorine shock can create too much chemical reaction in that system (Jacuzzi UK). If you use chlorine as the primary sanitizer, follow the shock product labeled for that system and your spa brand's instructions.

### Stabilizer and tablet cautions

CDC recommends against using cyanuric acid or chlorine products that contain cyanuric acid in hot tubs, and lists dichlor and trichlor as examples of chlorine products with cyanuric acid (CDC). That is CDC residential pool and hot tub guidance, not a spa-brand product ban by itself. Still read the product label and your spa manual before buying pool-style tablets or stabilized chlorine for a spa. Do not assume a pool product is appropriate because it contains "chlorine" in the name, and do not claim every trichlor product damages every hot tub unless your manual states that restriction.

## Manufacturer compatibility first

Before you buy a system:

1. Read the water-care section of your spa owner's manual.
2. Note whether the spa includes a brominator, mineral cartridge system, UV system, or salt system.
3. Confirm which sanitizers are allowed and which are prohibited for your model.

Jacuzzi's swim-spa maintenance guide states that some salt or alternative sanitizer options are model-specific and that unapproved systems can affect performance and warranty coverage (Jacuzzi). Check your own hot tub manual the same way: model rules win over generic internet advice.

Jacuzzi UK also mentions UV systems and salt-based systems as broader alternatives at a high level, while still requiring chemical sanitizer understanding for safe water (Jacuzzi UK). Jacuzzi UK states that salt systems convert salt to chlorine or bromine rather than eliminating disinfection chemistry entirely (Jacuzzi UK).

## Do not switch systems casually

Changing from chlorine to bromine, or the reverse, is not a simple bottle swap. Only follow a switch procedure if your spa manufacturer or dealer documents one for your model. If that procedure includes draining and refilling, follow those documented steps. Prefer the spa manufacturer's listed capacity when a product label needs gallons or liters; the [Hot Tub Volume Calculator](/calculators/hot-tub-volume-calculator) is only an estimate aid when the listed capacity is unknown.

If you are filling a new spa, Jacuzzi's beginner guide frames sanitizer choice as part of startup: pick the system, add sanitizer according to the startup product instructions, then maintain with ongoing testing (Jacuzzi). That is still not a reason to mix product chemistries later.

## Safe selection checks

1. **Open the owner's manual** and list allowed sanitizers and banned products.
2. **Choose one system** - chlorine or bromine - unless your manual documents another approved path.
3. **Buy a matching test kit** and learn its directions.
4. **Read every product label** for spa use, wait times, and incompatibilities.
5. **Store chemicals as labeled**, away from heat and moisture.
6. **Keep UV, mineral, or salt accessories within the spa maker's approved list.**

## When to stop and get help

Contact your dealer or a water-care professional if:

- the manual and a product label appear to conflict
- you are unsure whether a tablet, salt, or mineral system is approved for your spa
- water stays cloudy, foamy, or irritating after labeled corrections
