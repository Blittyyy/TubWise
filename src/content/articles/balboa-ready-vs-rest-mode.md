## Direct answer

On documented Balboa TP7 systems, **Ready**, **Rest**, and **Ready-in-Rest** are heat modes—not temperature ranges.

- **Ready (R):** Keeps water close to the set temperature around the clock. Balboa recommends Ready when you use the spa consistently (Balboa TP7 heat settings).
- **Rest:** Heats water during filter cycles. Balboa describes Rest for spas that are not used consistently (Balboa TP7 heat settings).
- **Ready-in-Rest (RR):** Same as Rest, except the spa can heat for **one hour** when needed after you turn on Jets 1 (Balboa TP7 heat settings).

A spa left in Rest can feel “broken” because it is not trying to hold set temperature all day.

## Safety note

Do not disable filtration or freeze protection to save energy. Do not set water hotter than your spa manufacturer allows. Balboa heat-mode guidance is not a promise of percentage energy savings. Follow your spa brand’s temperature limits and enter-water thermometer checks.

## Comparison table

| Mode | Typical TP7 indicator | Heating behavior (TP7 guide) | Often suitable when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ready | R | Maintains water close to set temperature 24 hours a day | Regular or daily use |
| Rest | Rest / IR-style Rest indication on the Heat screen legend | Heats during filter cycles | Infrequent use, accepting cooler water between cycles |
| Ready-in-Rest | RR | Like Rest, plus about one hour of heating when Jets 1 is turned on if heat is needed | Temporary convenience while remaining in a Rest-based strategy |

On the TP7 main screen legend, Balboa marks Ready as **R** and Ready-in-Rest as **RR** (Balboa). Exact iconography can vary by software and spa-brand overlay. Not every Balboa system offers every mode (Balboa main-screen note).

## Why Rest can look like a heating problem

In Rest, the spa is not tasked with holding set temperature continuously. Water can drift cooler between filter cycles even though the heater and sensors are working. That pattern is different from:

- [Why Does My Hot Tub Heat Only When the Jets Are On?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-heat-only-when-the-jets-are-on)
- [Why Is My Hot Tub Not Reaching the Set Temperature?](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-hot-tub-not-reaching-the-set-temperature)
- [Why Does My Hot Tub Temperature Keep Dropping?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-temperature-keep-dropping)

Check the heat mode before assuming the heater failed.

## Ready-in-Rest details on TP7

Balboa states Ready-in-Rest matches Rest, except it can heat for one hour when Jets 1 is turned on if heating is necessary (Balboa). Opening the Heat settings screen cancels Ready-in-Rest and returns the spa to Rest—even if you press no other buttons while on that screen (Balboa TP7 heat settings). That cancellation behavior is specific to the documented TP7 UI path.

## Heat modes are not temperature ranges

TP7 also has **High** and **Low** temperature ranges (often shown as **H** / **L**), which limit the set-temperature window (Balboa). Changing High/Low is separate from choosing Ready or Rest. Set temperature itself is adjusted on the set-temperature controls, not by renaming the heat mode (Balboa).

## Older Standard / Economy / Sleep wording

Legacy ML553 documentation uses **Standard**, **Economy**, and **Sleep**, plus a temporary **Standard-in-Economy** behavior after Jets 1 while in Economy (Balboa ML553). Those labels are historically similar in intent to “always maintain,” “heat mainly with filtration,” and reduced heating strategies, but TubWise does **not** claim perfect equivalence to Ready / Rest / Ready-in-Rest across every pack and software revision. Match the mode names printed in **your** manual.

## When it is probably not “just the mode”

Suspect a real heating or flow problem instead of Rest settings when:

- The spa is already in Ready and still will not warm over a normal heat-up window
- Flow, dry-heater, or other fault messages appear ([Balboa dr / dry](/error-codes/balboa/dr-dry), [Balboa Pr](/error-codes/balboa/pr))
- Breakers trip, or the spa never calls for heat with the cover on and set temperature above water temperature
