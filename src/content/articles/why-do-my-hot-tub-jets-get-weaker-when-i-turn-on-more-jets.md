## Direct answer

If jets feel acceptable when fewer outlets are open, then feel weaker as you open more jets or zones, the spa may be sharing a limited pump flow across more openings. A Hot Spot Collection owner's manual states that turning off flow to some jets increases flow to the others, and warns not to shut off more than half of those jets at the same time (Hot Spot Collection owner's manual, 2005). Sundance 680 Series manuals describe a massage selector that shares water in Combo position and sends maximum pressure to selected jet groups in other positions (Sundance 680 Series owner's manual). That pattern is different from jets that are weak in every setting.

Weaker flow with more jets open does not, by itself, prove a failing pump, a dirty filter, an air lock, blocked plumbing, or an electrical fault.

## Safety note

Use only topside jet faces, diverters, and air controls your manual describes. Do not force stiff controls. Do not remove jets or dismantle diverter valves. Do not open the equipment compartment or touch energized parts for this symptom. If weakness comes with grinding, overheating, burning smells, breaker trips, leaks near electrical equipment, or loss of circulation, shut power off and call a qualified spa technician.

## What this symptom is

This page is about **relative** change: strong enough with fewer open jets, then softer when more jets or zones open. It is not about:

- Steady weak flow in every mode → [Why Are My Hot Tub Jets Weak?](/troubleshooting/why-are-my-hot-tub-jets-weak)
- Rhythmic pulsing or surging → [Why Are My Hot Tub Jets Surging or Pulsating?](/troubleshooting/why-are-my-hot-tub-jets-surging-or-pulsating)
- Noise that appears only with jets on → [Why Does My Hot Tub Make Noise When the Jets Are On?](/troubleshooting/why-does-my-hot-tub-make-noise-when-the-jets-are-on)

Spa plumbing is brand- and model-specific. Confirm labels and allowed positions in your own manual.

## Expected flow sharing

### Opening more jets can soften each jet

Hot Spot Collection guidance says turning off some jet faces increases flow to the remaining open jets, and that shutting off more than half of those adjustable jets at once can damage plumbing and components (Hot Spot Collection owner's manual, 2005; Hot Spot Collection owner's manual, 2023). The reverse reading for a homeowner is simple: when more of those jets are open, the same pump output is spread across more outlets, so each open jet can feel softer.

That can be normal on spas with that design. It is not a pressure reading or a pump curve. Do not invent flow calculations.

### Diverters and zone selectors redirect water on purpose

On the Hot Spot Collection Pace model, a diverter lever sends water to different jet combinations (Hot Spot Collection owner's manual, 2023). Hot Spring Highlife manuals describe a SmartJet lever that diverts water to different jet combinations (Hot Spring Highlife owner's manual). On Sundance 680 McKinley and Ramona models, massage selector position A (Combo) shares water across jet systems, while positions B or C are used for maximum jet performance on selected groups during spa use (Sundance 680 Series owner's manual).

If one seat feels strong only when the selector is on that zone, and softer in Combo or with more groups open, that matches documented diversion behavior for those models. Other brands use different names and layouts.

### Air controls change feel without adding water

Hot Spot Collection manuals separate air control (air volume to jets) from jet faceplates that control water intensity (Hot Spot Collection owner's manual, 2005; 2023). Highlife manuals describe Comfort Control as an air adjustment for certain jets, while faceplates change jet pressure (Hot Spring Highlife owner's manual). Sundance 680 manuals say air controls introduce air into the water lines for specific jet groups (Sundance 680 Series owner's manual).

More air can make a jet feel punchier or noisier without increasing water volume. Do not treat an air-only change as proof that water flow rose.

### Pump speed changes the baseline

Jacuzzi describes most spa pumps as two-speed units, with low speed used for filtration and heating cycles (Jacuzzi). Compare the same jet layout on the same pump speed before deciding the spa has a fault.

## When the pattern is not simple sharing

Use [Why Are My Hot Tub Jets Weak?](/troubleshooting/why-are-my-hot-tub-jets-weak) when:

- Jets are weak even with fewer outlets open and diverters aimed at that group
- Strength dropped recently in every mode
- Water level is low or the filter is clogged (Jacuzzi lists dirty filters and low water as causes of weak flow)

Use [How to Tell if a Hot Tub Has an Air Lock](/troubleshooting/how-to-tell-if-a-hot-tub-has-an-air-lock) when the change started right after a drain and refill and flow is weak or absent while the pump runs.

Use [Why Are My Hot Tub Jets Surging or Pulsating?](/troubleshooting/why-are-my-hot-tub-jets-surging-or-pulsating) when the stream pulses or rises and falls instead of staying evenly softer.

## Safe topside checks

1. **Compare fewer open jets with more open jets** on the same pump speed. Softening only as more faces open fits the Hot Spot flow-sharing description (Hot Spot Collection owner's manual, 2005).
2. **Note diverter or massage-selector position.** Try the positions your manual documents. On covered Sundance 680 McKinley and Ramona spas, position A is for filtration; B or C are for maximum selected-jet performance during use (Sundance 680 Series owner's manual).
3. **Separate air from water.** Change only the air control, then only a jet face, and note which change alters the feel (Hot Spot; Highlife; Sundance 680).
4. **Do not shut most jets to chase power.** Hot Spot Collection manuals warn against turning off more than half of the adjustable hydromassage jets at once (Hot Spot Collection owner's manuals, 2005 and 2023). Sundance 680 manuals tell owners to keep at least six adjustable jets open for filtration characteristics (Sundance 680 Series owner's manual).
5. **If every mode is weak**, clean the filter and confirm water level, then follow [Why Are My Hot Tub Jets Weak?](/troubleshooting/why-are-my-hot-tub-jets-weak) and [How to Clean a Hot Tub Filter](/maintenance/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-filter).

## When to stop and call a technician

- Strength stays poor after you confirm documented diverter positions and reasonable jet-face settings
- Weakness is new in every mode after water-level and filter checks
- Controls are stuck and would need force or disassembly
- You hear grinding, smell burning, see leaks near electrical parts, or lose circulation
- Any next step would require equipment-bay or pump work

Those cases are outside topside flow-sharing checks.
